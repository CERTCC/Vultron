## Outcome

Idea #1254 requested a `create_inbound_error_followup_tree` factory in
`vultron/core/behaviors/inbox/`, porting the simulation's
`FollowUpOnErrorMessage` node. Planning recon reframed it to a **no-build**
decision, following the sibling #1253 precedent (a "workflow" that turned out
to be a documented non-thing).

## Why no code

- Core does not model the inbound error/inquiry message vocabulary
  (RE/EE/CE/GE/GI) the simulation node responds to — no emit path, no GI
  semantic in `vultron/semantic_registry/`. The responder would answer a
  stimulus core cannot produce.
- The underlying intent (surface and respond to a problem on a case) is already
  served by the note-to-case-via-ledger path (`vultron/core/behaviors/note/`,
  SYNC-02-002) plus out-of-band comms (out of scope).
- `FollowUpOnErrorMessage` was a stochastic fuzzer stub, not a modelled
  protocol exchange.

## Deliverables

- ADR-0049 (`docs/adr/0049-core-does-not-model-error-message-types.md`) records
  the boundary and the rejected seam-stub option (BT-16-001 / BT-18-004).
- `notes/bt-fuzzer-nodes-messaging.md` `FollowUpOnErrorMessage` placement
  flipped from `FUTURE:` to `RESOLVED:` (prevents re-planning).
- Registered in `docs/adr/index.md` and `mkdocs.yml`.
