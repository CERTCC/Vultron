## Problem

DEMOMA-17-002 is a MUST_NOT: "A change that consolidates demo code ... MUST NOT change what any demo scenario passes or fails on." Its rationale is about visibility — "if it also makes a check weaker or stronger, the change in the verdict is hidden inside a refactor, where reviewers do not look for it."

The evidence the project uses to discharge it cannot see that class of change at all. `notes/demo-scenario-tree.md` § "Verdicts do not move" names the recording as the evidence: an unchanged recording, or an isomorphic diff whose semantic-type sequence and entry count are unchanged. A recording holds protocol acts. It does not hold the scenario's own assertions — which replica a wait reads, whether a `demo_check` sits inside a `demo_gate`, which participant record a verification reads. A composition can change every one of those and the recording is byte-identical.

Measured on #5535 (PR #5595). That PR made four verification-side changes to `rccv-extension` and `rccv-handoff`; `export-demo-recordings --report-changes` run whole-tree exits 0, and the 11/11 demo-integration suite passes:

1. `rccv-extension`'s M7 `verify_publicly_disclosed` reads the Vendor's participant record rather than C1's. The function asserts VF and `pxa` only for VENDOR/DEPLOYER roles, so passing C1's record meant those assertions never ran. The check went from vacuous to real.
2. `rccv-handoff` gained the Reporter `EM.EXITED` wait before the Reporter publishes.
3. The roster the new owner's invite gates on is read from the **new** owner's replica (`owner_decision_story.py:552-553`), where `origin/main:rccv_handoff_demo.py:533-536` read the **old** owner's (`c1_client`). Different subject for the same gate.
4. `await_reporter_case_replica` — a `demo_check` — runs **before** the roster `demo_gate`, where `origin/main:rccv_handoff_demo.py:549-556` nested it **inside** that gate. A roster that never fills used to skip the check; now the check can fail the scenario first.

1 and 2 are disclosed in PR #5595's body. 3 and 4 were not, and were found only by reading the composed fragment against `origin/main`'s hand-placed form. 3 and 4 are also not fresh decisions: they are the ordering and the owner-parameterized roster read that the already-merged shared fragments use (`origin/main:coordinated_story.py:724`, `:785`, `rcvcv_demo.py:359`), so five scenarios already carry them. Every one of the four is a strengthening or a change of subject, none a weakening.

#5589 makes `--report-changes` a PR-time gate. That closes the reordered-fragment hole and does nothing for this one, because the recording is identical either way.

## Evidence or Context

- DEMOMA-17-002, and its rationale, in `specs/multi-actor-demo.yaml`.
- `notes/demo-scenario-tree.md` § "Verdicts do not move" — names the recording as the evidence.
- #5535 AC-3 binds a composition PR to DEMOMA-17-002 by name; #5536's AC will do the same, and #5536 reuses the two continuations #5535 extracted, so it inherits items 3 and 4 as given.

## Fix or Approach

Two parts, either of which is useful alone.

**A check that sees verification-side changes.** The composed fragments' waits and gates are reachable statically: an AST pass over `vultron/demo/helpers/` and `vultron/demo/scenario/` can record, per scenario path, the sequence of `demo_gate` / `demo_check` labels and, for each cross-container wait, which client expression it reads. Pin that as a committed artifact the way the recordings are pinned, so a composition PR that changes a check has to regenerate it and a reviewer sees the diff. This is the same shape as `scenario_tree.json`.

**A statement that says what the evidence is.** DEMOMA-17-002 obliges "no change to what a scenario passes or fails on" and the notes answer it with a recording. Either add a requirement naming the verification-side artifact as the second half of the evidence, or narrow DEMOMA-17-002 to the protocol acts and give the verification side its own MUST_NOT. Deciding which is the human call this issue asks for.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: a committed artifact records, per scenario, the gate/check labels and the client each cross-container wait reads, derived from the source and regenerated by a documented command.
- [ ] AC-2: a check fails when that artifact is stale, and it fails on an empty target set (DF-09-009).
- [ ] AC-3: the artifact is regenerated for the composed families on `main`, and the four changes above are either confirmed as intended or reverted, each with its reason recorded.
- [ ] AC-4: DEMOMA-17-002 and `notes/demo-scenario-tree.md` § "Verdicts do not move" name what the full evidence is, with no claim that an unchanged recording alone discharges the MUST_NOT.

Decisions (planned together with #5614 and #5615):

- The verification-side evidence is a runtime check tree captured during the in-process recording (through `_demo_accumulate` and the recorder's routed client), compared base vs head in the same CI job as #5614's ledger comparison. A static AST artifact was rejected because it cannot resolve which actor a reader expression names.
- DEMOMA-17-002 stays the behaviour rule; the gate gets its own DEMOCI-11 requirements. The rule was not split.
- A deliberate verdict change takes the `demo-verdict-change` label, which passes the job and still prints the diff.
- AC-3's four PR #5595 changes are confirmed as intended, not reverted: (1) M7 `verify_publicly_disclosed` reading the Vendor's record makes a vacuous check real; (2) the Reporter `EM.EXITED` wait before publishing is a correct causal gate; (3) the roster gate reading the new owner's replica follows EDF-06-002 (read the effect owner's own container) and matches the shared fragments five scenarios already use; (4) `await_reporter_case_replica` before the roster gate is that same shared ordering. All four are strengthenings or a change of subject toward the correct actor, and none is a weakening.
