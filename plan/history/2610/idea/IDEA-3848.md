## Observation

`AdrFrontmatter.supersedes` (`vultron/metadata/adr/schema.py`) is a single `NonEmptyStr | None`. ADR-0099 supersedes **two** ADRs (ADR-0017 and ADR-0082, retired in #3492 / PR #3847), so the successor side cannot record the relationship at all: it is recorded only as `superseded_by` on each retired ADR. ADR-0082 hit a smaller form of the same limit earlier — it *partially* superseded ADR-0062 and had to explain in prose that "the project has no `partially_supersedes` field".

MS-14-003 only requires the forward link (retired → successor), so nothing is broken. But `decision-audit` and readers of the successor ADR have no structured way to ask "what did this decision retire?", and the index generator cannot show it.

## Idea

Let `supersedes` (and `partially_supersedes`, if wanted for symmetry with `partially_superseded_by`) accept a list as well as a single filename, resolved by the same `_superseded_target_exists` check the loader applies to `superseded_by`. Optionally have `adr-index` render "supersedes X, Y" on the successor's line, and have `decision-audit` cross-check that every `superseded_by` pointer has a matching `supersedes` entry on the target.

Governing specs: MS-14-005 (frontmatter validates against the schema, pointers resolve); MS-14-006 (index generated from frontmatter).
