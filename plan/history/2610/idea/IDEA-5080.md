## Idea

The CASE_MANAGER uses the ledger position a participant states on an act to judge that act against what the participant knew when it acted.
The CASE_MANAGER can then tell "participant X made this decision knowing the case as of ledger position A", and treat a decision made on a stale replica as such.

## Why it matters

Today the CASE_MANAGER judges a participant's act against its own current case, with no record of what the participant knew when it acted.
An act made on a stale replica (for example, answering an embargo that was already revised) looks the same as one made on a current replica.

## What is already decided (not in scope here)

The stamp itself comes from #5099, under ADR-0155 and SYNC-03-004 (docs PR #5101):

- Every activity a participant sends the CASE_MANAGER about a case carries the sender's tail in a `ledgerTail` property (`LedgerPosition`, VAM-04-011), not in `content` or `context`.
- The CASE_MANAGER records the stated position as the peer's acknowledged position in `VultronReplicationState` and replays any lag, the same handling as `Read(CaseLedgerEntry)` (#5098).
- A position statement on its own is not a case event and is never ledgered (ADR-0152).

Those settle the original questions on which activities carry the stamp, where it goes on the wire, and whether it shares the record #5098 writes.
CM-11-011 (the full-case Invite reply carries the replier's position) is an existing instance of the same stamp.

## Open questions for planning

1. What the CASE_MANAGER does with an act whose stated position is behind its tail, beyond the replay: record only, warn, refuse, or apply a per-activity rule (for example, only for answers to an embargo revision the sender had not yet seen).
2. Whether the stated position is kept with the act's own ledger entry when the act is ledgered, so the ledger shows what each decision was made against.
