## Problem

The 2026-10-15 demo UI planning session settled that the demo scenarios form a **scenario tree** (see the **Demo** section of `CONTEXT.md`):

- The root is a Reporter who has found a vul and must choose whom to tell.
- Every node is a **branch point**: one role facing a decision.
- Every edge is a **choice**: a trigger that role calls.
- A **scenario** is one root-to-**ending** path. Its **recording** is the case ledgers it writes.

Who joins (report to a vendor or a coordinator; invite another vendor or a coordinator) and how a decision goes (validate or invalidate, extend or hand off, revise or terminate) are both just branch points.

Today the tree is implicit. It shows only in scenario names (`fv`, `fvv`, `fvcv-handoff`, `fcv-reject`, `rcv-embargo`, …), and sibling scenarios are full copies of each other. For example, `fcv_demo.py` and `fcv_reject_demo.py` share roughly 450 lines.
The interim plan is to declare branches on `@scenario` (ADR-0098); composition comes later (separate Idea).

## Decisions needed (interactive; do not build without them)

1. The declaration shape on `@scenario`: how a scenario names its parent, the branch point (role and decision), and the choice (trigger) it takes.
2. Where the presenter-facing label of a branch point ("As the Vendor, choose…") and of each choice lives.
3. The order of branch points along each existing path. For example, on the vendor path, does the Vendor validate before or after choosing whether to invite a coordinator?
4. How today's scenarios map onto one tree, including the root choice that `fv*` and `fc*` split on, and where `rcv-embargo` / `rcvv-embargo` sit relative to `fcv`.
5. Path-derived scenario names, folding in the Finder→Reporter rename (ADR-0078 retired `CVDRole.FINDER`): whether names become generated from the path, and the renaming plan for sub-commands, spec groups, and CI matrix entries.
6. The tree index artifact's format, which the UI's offline mode and live mode both read.
