## Problem

The occasion lookup table's "Sent by" column states the receiver's rule, and it may add the usual sender as `CVDRole` values (MSM-08-005). Some usual senders are not CVD roles, though. They are positions within one exchange: "the receiver of a report (usually a vendor or coordinator)", the invitee, the proposer, the actor a case proposal was addressed to. Today those can only appear as free prose in the occasion's description. That leaves the goal unmet that every "Sent by" cell names an identified, known role or position within a case.

## Why not just add `CVDRole.RECEIVER`

`CVDRole` values are durable facts stored on `CaseParticipant.roles`. A position is held only relative to one exchange: the same coordinator receives one report and sends the next. A report also comes before any case exists, so there is no participant record to hold it. Some positions already exist in code as `SenderEntitlementKind` members (`INVITEE`, `NAMED_ACTOR`), not as roles.

## Questions to settle

- Is there a closed vocabulary of exchange positions, and where does it live (beside `SenderEntitlementKind`, in the glossary, or both)?
- Does any position earn a place in `CVDRole` after all?
- Should the "usually" note accept positions as well as roles once the vocabulary exists?
