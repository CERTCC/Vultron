## Summary

The CASE_MANAGER should recompute an entry's hash before it fans the entry out or replays it, so a broken entry in its own store is caught where it starts.
Today a broken stored entry (the #3744 kind of bug) is found only after a replica refuses it (SYNC-03-006) and reports it (#5093, #5094).

## Why it matters

If the problem is on the send side, the check that finds it belongs on the send side too.
The receive-side check stays, because a problem can still show up first at a receiver, for example from a bug in rendering or parsing the wire form.

## Constraint

Use the shared `ledger_entry_hash_intact()` (`vultron/core/models/case_ledger.py`), the same predicate the receive side and the CASE_MANAGER's self-check (#5094) call. Do not add a second copy of the hashing logic.

## Open question

What should the CASE_MANAGER do with an entry that fails its own check: hold it back and log at ERROR, or send it anyway and log? Either way the replicas stall at that entry. The difference is where the error shows up and whether broken content leaves the CASE_MANAGER.
