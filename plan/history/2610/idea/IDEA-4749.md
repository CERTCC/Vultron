## Summary

Render the Protocol and Foundation requirement tiers from `specs/*.yaml` directly into the Protocol Specification pages, as generated or included content. Then the specification cannot state a requirement differently from the YAML.

## Motivation

The PR #4681 review settled that the YAML Protocol and Foundation tiers are the protocol's source of truth, and that the Protocol Specification presents them. Today the specification is mostly hand-written prose. It restates requirements in its own words and cites IDs inline (e.g. `docs/reference/vultron-spec/_syntactic-layer.md` has about 14 MUST/SHOULD statements and links like `[AKM-02-002](../specs/protocol.md#akm-02-002)`). Some MUST statements carry no ID at all, so they may have no YAML counterpart. Each restatement is a place the two can drift apart.

## Rough Approach

- Inventory every normative statement in `docs/reference/vultron-spec/_*.md`. Classify each as: matches a YAML entry, paraphrases one, or prose-only.
- Decide how requirements map to specification sections (by group ID, by a new section tag in the YAML, or otherwise). There may not yet be a good way to organize them by section; that's part of the work.
- Reconcile prose-only MUST statements: add them to the YAML, demote them to informative text, or remove them.
- Render requirements into the specification via `markdown-exec` / `render_for_kind`-style generation or `include-markdown` fragments, keeping hand-written narrative around them.
- This may warrant its own ADR (the source-of-truth rule and the rendering approach).
