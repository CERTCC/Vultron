## Summary

Issue #4293 records an embargo termination as two parts (ADR-0122, `docs/adr/0122-per-embargo-participant-consent.md`):

- **What:** `END_TIME_REACHED` or `EARLY`. It is carried on the wire as the termination `Remove(EmbargoEvent)`'s `content` and checked at the parse edge.
- **Why:** a structured cause that the `TERMINATED` register entry records beside the what. The only cause defined so far is `THREAT_SIGNAL`, with the specific signals `P` (public awareness), `X` (exploit public) and `A` (attacks observed). The shape is extensible: a cause is a model discriminated on `kind`, not a member of a closed list.

This Idea is the open question the maintainer left for later exploration in the #4293 decision (<https://github.com/CERTCC/Vultron/issues/4293#issuecomment-6170152980>).

## Open questions

1. **Which causes and details exist?** Candidates from `docs/topics/process_models/em/early_termination.md`:
   - `THREAT_SIGNAL` → `P` / `X` / `A` (defined);
   - a quorum of vendors at fix-ready (`VF····` for enough of them), which `ExitEmbargoWhenFixReady` already names as a reason;
   - fix deployed (`ExitEmbargoWhenDeployed`);
   - adversary awareness of the technical details, or adversaries holding exploit code;
   - a leak by a participant;
   - a participant's judgment (`ExitEmbargoForOtherReason`).

   Which of these deserve a named cause, and what details each carries (for example, which vendors make up the quorum)?
2. **Should the termination message ever carry the why?** Today the `Remove` does not: a replica that replays it records only the what, until #4294 copies whole register entries. (A copy of the case, such as a case snapshot's `embargoRegister`, already carries the entry with its cause.) Options: never (the cause is the CASE_MANAGER's record, copied by replay); or as a new property or a structured `content`, which ADR-0122 currently rules out ("no new AS2 property").
3. **Who may assert a cause?** The CASE_MANAGER only, or also the case owner's early `Remove`, for example a fix-ready quorum?
4. **Is a recorded cause required for every `EARLY` termination?** An early end with no cause recorded is allowed today.

Decisions: four cause kinds (`THREAT_SIGNAL` with signals, `FIX_READY` with the quorum's vendors, `FIX_DEPLOYED`, `OTHER`); the termination `Remove` carries the cause as `terminationCause` so replicas copy it from the ledger (ADR-0122 revision 7); the case owner's `Remove` may claim `FIX_READY`/`FIX_DEPLOYED`/`OTHER`, recorded as given, but a `THREAT_SIGNAL` there is refused in favour of a case status update setting P/X/A; a cause stays optional on the wire, and every Vultron-started early termination names one.
