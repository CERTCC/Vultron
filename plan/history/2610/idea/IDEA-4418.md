## Problem

There is no page that goes from "this happened in a CVD case" to "send this wire activity." The message reference pages open with tables keyed by formal shorthand (`RS`, `EP`, …), split across eight pages; the Protocol Quick Reference lists the formal message set without wire forms; about half the wire vocabulary has no shorthand, so a reader starting from a situation has nothing to look up by. Builders of other Vultron implementations need the wire messages to send — triggers are a prototype concept and are out of scope.

## Proposal (settled in a design interview, 2026-10-09; recorded in ADR-0128, proposed)

**The table.** One row per **occasion** — a situation in a case that a sender conveys with a wire activity, sometimes narrowed by a distinguishing field value or state context (glossary § Messaging and Protocol). `Add(ParticipantStatus)` carries vendor awareness, fix ready and fix deployed (`vf_state` / `d_state`), and `Add(CaseStatus)` carries public awareness, exploit public and attacks observed (`pxa_state`); a wire activity with several occasions appears once per occasion.

| When… | Send | Sent by | Distinguished by | Details |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a fix is ready | `Add(ParticipantStatus)[target=CaseParticipant]` | vendor | `vf_state` = `VF` | details (CF) |

- "When…" is first: readers scan by situation.
- "Send" is the literal AS2 summary (`Event`, not `EmbargoEvent`).
- The formal shorthand appears only in the Details link text.
- Groups follow message-page order: Report → Case proposal → Case & participants → Embargo → Case status → General, then Protocol mechanics (Faults & acknowledgments → Ledger replication). Rows within a group follow registry order. Every wire activity appears at least once.

**Source of truth is code; the YAML is a generated artifact.**

- `SemanticEntry.occasions: tuple[Occasion, ...]` (≥1, constructor-checked). `Occasion` (frozen) holds: when, distinguishing value, stable anchor ID, description, example function, optional how-to link, optional shorthand, optional notes.
- Derived, never typed: wire summary (from the `ActivityPattern`), sent by (from the use case's `sender_entitlement`; exempt shows "not yet checked"), page/group (on the entry), `MappingStatus` and discriminator (from the occasions).
- One generator writes a committed YAML (with `format_version`; internal, not published — publication is #2959) and committed `include-markdown` fragments: whole table, per-page slice, one per occasion. A pre-commit `--check` hook gates both. `_mapping.py` becomes the YAML's Pydantic loader/validator.
- Fragments, not `markdown_exec`: exec-printed `.md` links skip MkDocs link rewriting (vultron/metadata/AGENTS.md § Generate vs. Check).

**Pages.**

- `docs/reference/messages/index.md` opens with the table; the two-vocabularies explanation moves below.
- Each message page: its slice, then one generated section per occasion with a stable meaning-level anchor (`{#fix-ready}`); hand-written intros/addenda around the includes. Pages may be restructured to fit.
- How-to guides include the occasion fragment where a step sends it, JSON collapsed. Reference sections list "Seen in", derived from include directives.
- Quick Reference keeps its formal table, links to the occasion table.
- `fv-demo-protocol.md` hand-written semantics tables become embeds or links.

**Checks.** Committed artifacts equal regeneration; every non-exempt `MessageSemantics` has ≥1 occasion; distinguishing values are real enum values; anchor IDs unique and resolve to exactly one section; every occasion section is referenced; each occasion's example dispatches to its own entry (the exemption map in `test_vocab_examples_dispatchable.py` is empty today).
