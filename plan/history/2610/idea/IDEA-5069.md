## Summary

Review the draft page `docs/topics/measuring_cvd/process_model_desiderata.md` (PR #5067) and settle which candidate desiderata to keep, change or drop.

## Motivation

The page was drafted from a survey of the specs, notes and state modules, and its candidates have not been reviewed by the people who own the measurement model. Points that need a decision:

- Whether to split embargo exit by cause (on its own terms vs. ended by a threat signal) and public awareness by source (a participant's publication vs. a leak). Only the first split is recorded today (EMB-13-004).
- Whether "every vendor's fix is ready before the embargo ends" should be the desideratum, given that VP-09-007 allows ending at a quorum.
- Whether "a deployer joins before the fix it deploys is ready" belongs here, since the protocol does not require deployers to be invited.
- Whether "embargo in force before public awareness" is worth keeping, given that every case starts with an embargo, so it mostly measures whether the case forms before disclosure.
- How to handle desiderata that imply one another (accept-before-P implies V-before-P) in a summed skill score.

## Rough Approach

Walk through the page section by section in a grilling session, then edit the page in place.
