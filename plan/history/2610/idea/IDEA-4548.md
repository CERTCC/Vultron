## Idea

When a participant's embargo consent moves to `DECLINED` or `TIMED_OUT`, the
CASE_MANAGER could send the case owner a separate notification activity.

CM-28-005 requires a CASE_MANAGER-authored ledger entry for an expiry, so the
owner can already see it via the ledger. The open question was whether a
separate notification is worth adding on top of that, and if so for which
transitions.

## Outcome

No separate notification. The decline or expiry entry is the owner's
notification: an active owner receives its `Announce(CaseLedgerEntry)` fan-out,
and an owner holding CASE_MANAGER already holds the entry (MSM-07-007 amended).

Planning found the real gap: expiry is evaluated only on a late `Accept` and at
owner close, so an invitee that never answers stays `INVITED` until the case
closes. CM-28-015 adds a sweep of overdue invitations before the owner's embargo
decision and on every received embargo activity, after the sender gate.
