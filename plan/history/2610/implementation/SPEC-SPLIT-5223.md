`specs/meta-specifications.yaml`, `specs/spec-registry.yaml`,
`specs/history-management.yaml` and `specs/notes-frontmatter.yaml` (MS-03-001).

statements that now hold its obligations. The original ID keeps the main obligation, and
each other obligation is a new ID. A reviewer confirms that every obligation in the old
text appears in exactly one new statement. The PR body gives the reason for each row
under `## Spec amended`.

Kept whole: SR-13-013 carries `lint_suppress: [compound_statement]`, because its two
clauses state one rule from its two ends: record `refines` on the narrower requirement,
and no `constrains` on the general one.
