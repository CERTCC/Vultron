Implemented ADR-0114 for #4048. The CASE_MANAGER now records an inert
CaseParticipant (joined=False) at RM.RECEIVED the moment it sends a stub
Invite, before the invitee replies (CM-11-006).

On Accept(Invite): the inert record is activated (joined=True) and VF
advances to Vf for VENDOR invitees (CM-11-009). On Reject(Invite): the
inert record moves to RM.CLOSED and is kept as history; PEC DECLINED is
applied when an active embargo is in force (CM-11-007).
