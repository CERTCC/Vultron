`vultron/core/predicates/case_closure.py` is now the one "case is fully closed"
condition (`ledger_records_full_closure`, `CASE_FULLY_CLOSED_EVENT_TYPE`). The
close-case tree, the post-close guard, the demo milestones and the CI invariant
all use it, and no handler has its own guard.

Specs: VP-03-013 covers every message class (R*, EM, CS, GI) and states the
CASE_MANAGER kill switch; RMB-01-005 and RMB-14-002 point non-R* traffic to
VP-03-013 and CM-23-013. CM-23-013 lets the CASE_MANAGER keep answering resync
by replay (which appends nothing), and the resync tree is exempt from the guard.
The closure Announce is emitted before writes cease (tested).
