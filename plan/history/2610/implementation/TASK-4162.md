Every received tree now carries `CaseOpenToExternalWritesNode` right after
intake. At the case's CASE_MANAGER, once `case_fully_closed` is on its ledger,
the tree records nothing, changes no case or participant state, and reports
`REFUSED`; the drop is logged at INFO with the activity id, sender actor id and
case id (CM-23-013). Replicas pass; resync and the close-case inner arm are the
closed set of exemptions (`PostCloseExemption`). Embargo Accept and Invite ask
the guard first through the `door_refusal` preflight, because they commit or
send before their tree runs.

Reply policy (user decision, ADR-0131, CM-23-017): one policy point,
`ActorConfig.post_close_reply`, default `drop`. With `reject`, only a late
`Leave(VulnerabilityCase)` gets `Reject(Leave(VulnerabilityCase))`; every other
late activity is dropped with no reply under both settings, because a Reject
that wraps it already means a declined decision.

The ADR was renumbered 0129 -> 0131 at rebase (main took 0129, open PR #4452
claims 0130) and carries `accepted-provisional` to match its epoch (MS-14-007).
