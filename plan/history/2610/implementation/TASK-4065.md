A non-owner Leave that arrives after `case_fully_closed` now reports `REFUSED`
under both `post_close_reply` settings and emits a Reject only under `reject`.
The post-close guard sits ahead of `CloseOrDecline`, so the receipt commit never
runs and no `close_case` entry is committed or fanned out. The strict xfail on
`test_bystander_leave_after_owner_close_is_refused` was removed; the pending
Invite expiry case stays xfailed (#4066).

Docstrings in `nodes/leave/record.py`, `nodes/leave/advance.py` and the RM-closed
ordering test now call `case_fully_closed` the write boundary.
`manage_case.md` says a late Leave records nothing and gets no reply by default.
A whole-scenario CI invariant (18) checks each demo's Case Actor ledger for
participant acts after `case_fully_closed`, reusing `case_closure_violations`.
