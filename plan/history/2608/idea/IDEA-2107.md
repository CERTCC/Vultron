## Summary

Open Question #4 in `docs/reference/draft-vultron-spec.md`: how much CVD
background material belongs inline in the RFC vs. a separate companion
"CVD Concepts" document?

## Resolution

Direction A — admonition-block placeholders in §1.1 and §2.1 of the draft
spec, linking to the existing `docs/topics/background/` pages. Since the
draft spec is hosted on the same MkDocs site, hyperlinks to the background
pages are sufficient; no separate companion document is needed.

§1.1 now contains a framing paragraph plus an `!!! info` admonition that
links to:

- [CVD as a Coordination Problem](../topics/background/index.md)
- [What Does Success Mean in CVD?](../topics/background/cvd_success.md)
- [The Need for Interoperability](../topics/background/interoperability.md)
- CERT Guide to Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (external)

§2.1 has a framing sentence plus an `!!! info` admonition pointing to
`docs/reference/glossary.md` and `vultron/enums/roles.py`.

Open Question #4 in the spec is marked resolved with a forward reference to #2698.
