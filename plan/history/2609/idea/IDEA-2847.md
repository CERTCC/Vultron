## Why these are grouped

Two issues, directly coupled: #665 is already a child of #533, and it exists to supply the
transport the UI needs. Unrelated to everything else in the backlog, which is why this is a
small standalone group rather than folded elsewhere.

## Members

- **#533 — interactive ReactFlow demo UI with CYOA scenarios for stakeholder
  presentations.** The parent. Note the audience: stakeholder presentation, not developer
  tooling — that shapes every scope decision.
- **#665 — add an SSE stream endpoint for live case log updates.** The transport #533
  needs. Also independently useful for observability, so it may be worth building even if
  the UI is deferred.

  legitimate outcome** for a presentation artifact.
- A verdict on #665 independent of #533 — the SSE endpoint may stand on its own
  observability merits.
- Task set parented to #676.
