## Summary

ADR-0099 detail 7, as amended in #3888, makes an unrecognised key on a **core object** a refusal: `CoreObject` resolves `extra="forbid"` (ARCH-12-003), so the sender learns of the key from the 422 and nothing is dropped silently.

The **activity envelope** is the exception. `as_Base` keeps Pydantic's default `extra="ignore"`, so an unknown key on the envelope itself (`{"type": "Offer", "actor": ..., "fooBar": 1}`) is discarded with no log line and no refusal. Measured on 2026-09-29: `as_Activity.model_validate({..., "fooBar": 1}).model_extra` is `None`.

That is the last place the #2232 shape can still happen: a key vanishing between the wire and the reader without a trace. It is out of `extra="forbid"`'s reach for a reason — the envelope is where an AS2-extending peer's properties land, and refusing the whole message for one unknown envelope key is a federation cost detail 7 deliberately does not pay there.

## Idea

Report, do not refuse: log every unknown envelope key at INFO with `activity_id` and `actor_id`, keyed on the envelope class, so an operator can see what peers are sending that Vultron ignores. The original detail 8 also proposed a WARNING when the key is a near miss for a declared field or alias after lowercasing and stripping non-alphanumerics, or matches a retired name; that deterministic check (no edit-distance matching) is still the right shape if reporting is built.

Where: a `mode="wrap"` validator on `as_Base`, or `model_extra` inspection in `parse_activity`, whichever keeps the wire root free of core imports (ARCH-22 allow-list).
