The governing rule is that a priority gate names a tier, not a keyword (MS-02-003,
with MS-02-004 for code).
MUST_NOT is gated like MUST (MS-10-003), and SHOULD_NOT like SHOULD (SR-11-004).

Missing-verification ratchets work as follows:

- Each kind's ceiling equals its live count, and every non-zero ceiling names an
  owning issue (MS-10-006).
- Suppressed items count toward the ceiling.
- A relabel carries its verification clause (MS-10-008).
- At zero, the check becomes a hard error and the ceiling entry is deleted
  (MS-10-007), and the hard error cannot be suppressed.

SR-11-003 stays MUST-only for now as a recorded exception. MS-12-007 forbids new
suppressions, so #3601 adjudicates the 139 protocol MUST_NOTs that have no story
references, and #2717 then flips the gate.

Measured on 2026-09-24, requirements missing `verification:`: 1180 MUST, and 241
of 305 MUST_NOT (114 of them protocol).
