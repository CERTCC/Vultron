## Why these are grouped

Nine scenario issues, and most of them say some version of "this would require
protocol primitives that do not exist yet." **This session's primary job is a sort,
not a design:** which scenarios are pure authoring work against today's protocol, and
which are actually protocol-gap reports wearing scenario clothing.

That sort is cheap and high-value — it will reclassify a lot of what currently sits in
`Someday` and stop each scenario from being individually re-analyzed later. It is why
this group stayed on the `soon` side of the #2828 split even though every member's own
tier still reads `Someday`: the triage is what tells us which of them are reachable.
