## Summary

Conduct a deep-pass triage review of three borderline notes files to determine
whether each belongs in the public Explanation quadrant, the How-to quadrant,
the Future Work section, or should be skipped. Then write or update the

## Motivation

These three files were identified as borderline during the triage in #2526:
they contain protocol-level concepts but also have significant Python/BT
implementation prose mixed in. A focused review is needed to determine
whether there is enough language-neutral content to justify promotion, and
if so, which docs quadrant is the right home.
