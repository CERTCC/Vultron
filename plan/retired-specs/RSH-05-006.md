# RSH-05-006 (retired)

- **Retired**: 2026-10-20 (#3741)
- **Replacement**: RSH-09-003
- **Why**: ADR-0107 detail 5: a received status is accepted whole or refused whole, so the ledger never records a status nobody sent (#4451 decision on #3741). RM.CLOSED stays terminal for every status a closed participant sends.
- **Last text**:

```yaml
  - id: RSH-05-006
    priority: MUST
    kind: protocol
    statement: >
      `RM.CLOSED` MUST be treated as terminal for the `rm` dimension only. A
      status that reaches `RM.CLOSED` MUST still have its accepted values in
      other dimensions recorded. A status update from a participant whose
      closure the case's ledger already records is refused in full instead
      (CM-23-018).
    rationale: >
      A vendor that closes its report management workflow may deploy a fix
      (VFD) or observe public disclosure (PXA) as it closes. Freezing the whole
      snapshot at RM closure discards real protocol state. Once its closure is
      recorded the participant has left the case and may act on nothing
      further (CM-23-004, CM-23-018), so it reports that progress before it
      leaves.
    verification: Unit tests in `test/core/behaviors/status/test_partial_accept_participant_status.py` confirm a status
      that reaches `RM.CLOSED` still has its accepted VFD dimension value recorded, and that a later update from a
      participant already at `RM.CLOSED` is refused in full.
    stories:
    - story_2022_088
    - story_2022_107
```
