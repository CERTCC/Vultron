# CM-21-018 (retired)

- **Retired**: 2026-10-20 (#4939)
- **Why**: ADR-0148: the CASE_MANAGER makes the one ownership-transfer Offer under its own id, so there is no forward and no inReplyTo back-link to resolve; an answer names that Offer directly (CM-21-005).
- **Last text**:

```yaml
  - id: CM-21-018
    priority: MUST
    kind: protocol
    statement: >-
      The `Offer(VulnerabilityCase)` the CASE_MANAGER forwards to the transferee
      (CM-21-005) MUST carry `inReplyTo` set to the id of the recorded Offer it
      forwards. When an Accept or Reject names a forward, the CASE_MANAGER MUST
      resolve it to the recorded Offer through that link, read from the forward it
      sent and stored, and MUST NOT resolve it by comparing times. An answer naming
      a forward that carries no `inReplyTo`, or whose link names no recorded Offer
      for that case and transferee, MUST be refused as a protocol error, with no
      ledger commit and no outbound activity.
    rationale: >-
      An answer closes exactly the Offer it answers (CM-21-014), and both the Accept
      and the Reject commit. A forward that names nothing it stands for leaves the
      CASE_MANAGER guessing: matching by time lets a late answer to one Offer close
      a newer Offer to the same transferee recorded in the same second. `inReplyTo`
      is the AS2 field that points one activity at the one it follows from, and
      `context` is not free to carry the link: it already names the case.
    verification: >-
      A test marked `CM-21-018` runs the forward and asserts its `inReplyTo` is the
      recorded Offer's id; a test delivers an answer to an earlier Offer's forward
      after a later Offer to the same transferee is recorded in the same second and
      asserts the later Offer stays open; a test delivers an answer naming a forward
      with no `inReplyTo`, and one linked to another transferee's Offer, and asserts
      each is refused with no ledger commit and no outbound activity.
    relationships:

      spec_id: CM-21-005

      spec_id: CM-21-014
```
