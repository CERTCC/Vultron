# EMB-15-002 (retired)

- **Retired**: 2026-11-12 (#6307)
- **Replacement**: EMB-15-013
- **Why**: The Case Owner approval gate it required in an invitee's own answer had nothing to approve: the answer is the invitee's own consent row (CM-18, ADR-0122), and a gate asking another actor is a protocol ask (BT-18-014).
