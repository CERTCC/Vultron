# RSH-05-008 (retired)

- **Retired**: 2026-10-20 (#3741)
- **Replacement**: RSH-09-003
- **Why**: ADR-0107 detail 5: a received status is accepted whole or refused whole, so the ledger never records a status nobody sent (#4451 decision on #3741). The embedded EM is still not judged against the case; the rule moved into the acceptance rules.
- **Last text**:

```yaml
  - id: RSH-05-008
    priority: SHOULD
    kind: protocol
    statement: >
      The `em` dimension SHOULD NOT be adjudicated at StatusAdoptionGate; embargo state
      belongs to EmbargoTeardownAuthorizationGate (`add_case_status_tree`).
    rationale: >
      EM is per-case rather than per-participant and its authorization gate is
      the EmbargoTeardownAuthorizationGate (RSH-02-001). Adjudicating it in the StatusAdoptionGate guard
      would duplicate — and could contradict — EmbargoTeardownAuthorizationGate's decision.
    note: >
      EmbargoTeardownAuthorizationGate EM adjudication is tracked in ISSUE-2256.
```
