  - id: RMB-01-005
    priority: MAY
    kind: protocol
    statement: >-
      A Participant in RM Closed (q^rm ∈ C) receiving any R* message MAY
      ignore it without sending a response. The same holds for non-R* traffic
      (EM, CS, GI) on a closed case (VP-03-013); for the CASE_MANAGER once the
      case is fully closed, ignoring is not optional: it records nothing
      (CM-23-013).
    rationale: >-
      Closed cases are terminal. The protocol permits silent discard to avoid
      generating unnecessary traffic for completed work. The R* scope is this
      group's trigger, not the limit of the rule: VP-03-013 covers every message
      class, and ADR-0085 makes owner close a hard kill switch at the
      CASE_MANAGER.
