# RSH-05-016 (retired)

- **Retired**: 2026-10-20 (#3741)
- **Replacement**: RSH-09-005
- **Why**: ADR-0107 detail 5: a received status is accepted whole or refused whole, so the ledger never records a status nobody sent (#4451 decision on #3741). A refused CaseStatus dimension refuses the whole status; nothing is carried forward.
- **Last text**:

```yaml
  - id: RSH-05-016
    priority: MUST
    kind: protocol
    statement: >
      When an EM or PXA dimension of a received `CaseStatus` is refused, the
      receiver MUST record the case's current value for that dimension alongside
      the accepted value from the other dimension, as a single `CaseStatus`.
    rationale: >
      The recorded status must remain a coherent snapshot of the receiver's
      view of the case. Omitting the refused dimension would leave it undefined;
      recording the asserted value would adopt a claim the receiver just refused.
      This is the CaseStatus parallel to RSH-05-002 for ParticipantStatus.
    verification: >
      Unit tests confirm that when the EM dimension is refused, the resulting
      recorded `CaseStatus` carries the case's current EM state, not the
      asserted EM state.
    note: >
      Implemented by `FilterCsEmDimensionNode` carrying forward `current_em`
      and `FilterCsPxaDimensionNode` carrying forward `current_pxa` in
      `vultron/core/behaviors/status/nodes/cs_dimension_filter.py`.
    lint_suppress:
    - missing_story_reference
```
