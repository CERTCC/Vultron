# EMB-17-011 (retired)

- **Retired**: 2026-10-22 (#5039)
- **Replacement**: EMB-17-003
- **Why**: Its premise is reversed: the re-invite has no semantic type of its own. ADR-0152 makes it a relayed Invite(EmbargoEvent), attributedTo the embargo's proposer and committed as invite_to_embargo_on_case, which a replica applies through the relay slot; EMB-17-003 now states that.
- **Last text**:

```yaml
  - id: EMB-17-011
    priority: MUST
    kind: protocol
    statement: >-
      When the CASE_MANAGER re-invites a late accepter to the case's current
      embargo (EMB-17-003), it MUST commit the fresh `Invite(EmbargoEvent)` as a
      distinct ledger entry of semantic type `invite_to_embargo_on_case_reinvite`
      once it has queued the Invite (SYNC-09-002), and a replica MUST replay the entry by
      applying the invitee's PEC `INVITE` and recording the Invite's `endTime`
      as its RSVP deadline, without moving EM state (CM-28-013, RSH-08-004).
    rationale: >-
      Without a committed entry the replica never learns the participant was
      re-invited, so its record keeps the lapsed state and deadline while the
      CASE_MANAGER's shows the fresh ones. The re-invite has no proposer, so the
      relayed-Invite classifier would read a committed copy as a proposal and
      move EM state for an embargo that is already current; a semantic type of its
      own gets a replay slot that cannot do that (EP-09-007, ADR-0118).
    verification: >-
      `test_the_managers_reinvite_is_committed_and_replayed_by_a_replica` in
      `test/core/use_cases/received/test_embargo_answer_routing.py` drives
      the full path across per-actor stores: the late Accept reaches the
      CASE_MANAGER, the re-invite entry is committed and broadcast, and the
      replica reads the invitee `INVITED` with the manager's deadline and the
      manager's EM state. `test_embargo_relay_entries_are_replayed.py`
      classifies the entry shape.
    testable: true
    postconditions:
    - description: >-
        A ledger entry with semantic type `invite_to_embargo_on_case_reinvite` is
        committed in the CASE_MANAGER's store and replayed in every replica's
        store; the invitee reads `INVITED` with the Invite's deadline on each,
        and no EM state moves
    relationships:

      spec_id: RSH-08-004
    - rel_type: depends_on
      spec_id: CM-28-013
    adr:
    - ADR-0118
    stories:
    - story_2022_013
```
