# CM-14-006 (retired)

- **Retired**: 2026-11-06 (#5886)
- **Replacement**: EP-04-003
- **Why**: Duplicated EP-04-003's shorter-of-two rule at SHOULD where EP-04-003 is MUST, so a reader of case initialization could weaken a mandatory outcome (#5886).
- **Last text**:

```yaml
  - id: CM-14-006
    priority: SHOULD
    kind: protocol
    statement: >-
      If the reporter included an embargo proposal with their
      `Offer(VulnerabilityReport)`, the case initialization SHOULD reconcile the
      reporter's proposed duration against the CASE_OWNER's published embargo policy
      (its actor default, EP-04-003) and initialize the embargo at the shorter of the
      two durations
    rationale: >-
      An agreement to an embargo of N days is also an agreement to every shorter
      embargo. Thus the shorter of the two terms is the term that both the reporter and
      the Case Owner accepted (EP-04-003). The longer term would bind a party to terms
      that it did not offer.
    note: >-
      EP-04-004 now specifies the mechanism: the reporter embeds a proposed `EmbargoEvent` in the
      `Offer(VulnerabilityReport)` activity. This requirement is therefore reachable, and the
      reconciliation it describes is the shortest-wins comparison of EP-04-003. Note that the
      protocol default of EP-04-005 is not a candidate in that comparison (EP-04-006). Amended by
      ADR-0096; see `notes/embargo-default-semantics.md` § "Reporter Embargo Proposal
      Mechanism (EP-04-004)".

      spec_id: CM-14-001

      spec_id: EP-04-004
```
