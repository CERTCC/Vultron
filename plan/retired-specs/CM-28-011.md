# CM-28-011 (retired)

- **Retired**: 2026-11-05 (#5858)

- **Why**: Restated EP-07-011 for the CM-18-023 policy-window path, which EP-07-011 already names, and the second sentence of CM-28-019; the clamp of a policy-window deadline to the embargo's end_time is stated once, in EP-07-011.
- **Last text**:

```yaml
  - id: CM-28-011
    priority: MUST_NOT
    kind: protocol
    statement: >-
      The CM-18-023 policy window MUST NOT be applied so as to place the effective RSVP
      deadline after the embargo's own `end_time`. Where the window would overshoot, the
      deadline is the embargo's `end_time`.
    rationale: >-
      The policy window is a fixed duration (by default, 7 days) from the `published`
      timestamp of the invitation. It does not refer to the embargo that the invitation
      concerns. On a short embargo, the window causes a pocket veto after the embargo
      ends. It records an expiry for an invitation whose embargo is already over.
      EP-07-006 gives the general rule. This requirement applies it to the specific
      fallback path that CM-18-023 defines. See ADR-0096.
    verification: >-
      A unit test confirms that when the CM-18-023 policy window would place the deadline
      after the embargo's `end_time`, the embargo's `end_time` is used instead.
    relationships:
    - rel_type: refines
      spec_id: EP-07-006
    - rel_type: uses_definition
      spec_id: CM-18-023
    adr:
    - ADR-0096
```
