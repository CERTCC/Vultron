  - id: BT-10-010
    priority: SHOULD
    kind: protocol
    statement: >-
      The CASE_MANAGER SHOULD leave the invitee's RM state at RM.RECEIVED when the
      invitee accepts the stub Invite (CM-11-001).
    rationale: >-
      When the invitee accepts the stub Invite, it only asks for the full case. It does
      not say if the report is valid. The invitee's replies to the full-case Invite
      drive its triage (RM.VALID, RM.INVALID or RM.CLOSED, CM-11-011). Then
      `Join(VulnerabilityCase)` or `Ignore(VulnerabilityCase)` moves it to RM.ACCEPTED
      or RM.DEFERRED. Thus the record of the CASE_MANAGER does not show a decision that
      the invitee has not made.
    verification: >-
      `test_accept_invite_participant_recorded_at_rm_received` in
      `test/core/use_cases/received/actor/test_invite.py` asserts that an accepted
      Invite records the participant at RM.RECEIVED only.
