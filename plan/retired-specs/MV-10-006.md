# MV-10-006 (retired)

- **Retired**: 2026-11-05 (#5858)
- **Replacement**: CM-11-001
- **Why**: It restates CM-11-001's clause that accepting the stub Invite under an active embargo marks the participant's consent row AGREED.
- **Last text**:

```yaml
  - id: MV-10-006
    priority: MUST
    kind: protocol
    statement: >-
      When a participant accepts the stub Invite
      (`Accept(Invite(Actor)[target=VulnerabilityCaseStub])`) and the case has an active
      embargo, the acceptance MUST
      imply consent to the active embargo, marking that participant's consent row
      for it `AGREED` (CM-11-001).
    rationale: >-
      An invitee uses the stub to decide whether to join. To join a case under an active
      embargo is to take part under that embargo. When the acceptance counts as consent,
      no participant becomes active without a recorded answer to the embargo. The
      delivery gate of MV-10-005 relies on this.
    verification: >-
      `test_accept_invite_actor_to_case_records_active_embargo` in
      `test/core/use_cases/received/actor/test_invite.py` confirms that accepting the
      stub Invite while an embargo is active marks the accepting participant's consent
      row for that embargo `AGREED`.

      spec_id: CM-11-001
```
