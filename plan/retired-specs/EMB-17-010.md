# EMB-17-010 (retired)

- **Retired**: 2026-10-21 (#5038)
- **Replacement**: EMB-17-004
- **Why**: ADR-0152 ruling 3: a late Accept with no embargo to bind changes nothing at any receiver, so the CASE_MANAGER ledgers and sends nothing for it; the no-op Ignore entry this required is removed, and EMB-17-004 now states the no-op
- **Last text**:

```yaml
  - id: EMB-17-010
    priority: MUST
    kind: protocol
    statement: >-
      When the CASE_MANAGER acknowledges a no-op late Accept (EMB-17-004), it
      MUST commit a distinct ledger entry of semantic type
      `invite_to_embargo_on_case_expired_noop` so that replicas can record the
      acknowledgement without applying any PEC transition (RSH-08-004). Its
      payloadSnapshot MUST be the CASE_MANAGER's own factory-built
      `Ignore(Accept(Invite(EmbargoEvent)))` carrying the late Accept inline,
      in the AS2 shape a received activity is recorded in (CLP-07-009,
      CLP-07-013, VM-08-003).
    rationale: >-
      Without a committed entry the replica has no record that the late Accept
      was received and acknowledged; its chain position would be missing,
      silently blocking replay of all subsequent entries (SYNC-12-001,
      ADR-0118).
      Carrying the late Accept inline is a protocol rule, not this prototype's
      inline-object shortcut: it is ledger snapshot content, which the hash
      chain covers only when carried in full (CLP-07-006).
    verification: >-
      `test_the_managers_noop_decision_is_committed_and_replayed_by_a_replica`
      drives the full path across per-actor stores: the late Accept reaches the
      CASE_MANAGER with EM EXITED, the noop entry is committed and broadcast,
      the replica's participant PEC is unchanged, and the replica's ledger
      contains the noop entry (EMB-17-004, RSH-08-004).
    testable: true
    postconditions:
    - description: >-
        A ledger entry with semantic type `invite_to_embargo_on_case_expired_noop`
        is committed in the CASE_MANAGER's store and replayed in every
        replica's store; no PEC transition is applied on any store
    relationships:
```
