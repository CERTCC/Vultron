# CM-28-004 (retired)

- **Retired**: 2026-11-05 (#5858)
- **Replacement**: CM-18-002
- **Why**: Restated CM-18-002, which the per-embargo consent-row group owns; the rule that an invite lapse records TIMED_OUT is stated once, and CM-18-002 now carries CM-28-004's stub-Invite note and its late-Accept rationale.
- **Last text**:

```yaml
  - id: CM-28-004
    priority: MUST
    kind: protocol
    statement: >-
      The CASE_MANAGER MUST record an expired invitation as the consent row's
      `TIMED_OUT` state, by the `TIME_OUT` trigger (`INVITED → TIMED_OUT`), never as
      `DECLINED`.
    rationale: >-
      A participant that did not answer made no decision, and code, logs, demos and replicas read
      the consent row, not ledger provenance (ADR-0118). An expired participant is re-invitable
      (CM-18-003), excluded from embargoed content like any non-signatory
      (CM-10-004), and returned to `AGREED` by a late Accept the CASE_MANAGER
      honours — a signatory only if that embargo is in force (EMB-17-002,
      MSM-07-003). ADR-0118 reverses ADR-0065's choice to record a lapsed
      invitation as `DECLINED`.
    note: >-
      "Invitation" here is an `Invite(EmbargoEvent)`. An expired stub Invite
      (`Invite(Actor)[target=VulnerabilityCaseStub]`) carries embargo terms but changes
      no participant state, consent included (CM-11-040): an unanswered stub is
      not a refusal to join, and the CASE_MANAGER may re-invite on the same
      record (CM-11-015, ADR-0114).
    verification: >-
      A unit test confirms that the CASE_MANAGER's expiry evaluation moves an `INVITED` participant
      past its deadline to `TIMED_OUT`, not `DECLINED`.
    relationships:

      spec_id: CM-18-003

      spec_id: CM-28-005
```
