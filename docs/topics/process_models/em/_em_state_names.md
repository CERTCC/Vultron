!!! note "State names"

    The Vultron Protocol Specification names two of the embargo states *Revised* and *Exited* ([§7.1 States](../../../reference/vultron-spec/tracking-models.md#71-states)).
    The process-model pages call the same two states *Revise* and *eXited*, so that the capital letter in each name gives its one-letter shorthand, R and X.
    Both sets of names stay in use; they name the same states.
