# User Story `2026_087`

<!-- story-start -->As a Case Owner, I want my acceptance or rejection of an embargo proposal to decide the case's embargo, and every answer to an embargo invitation, my own included, to record only the answerer's consent, so that the case has one embargo in force at a time and everyone knows who decided it<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

The Case Owner's `Accept` or `Reject` of the embargo itself is the decision; an answer to the invitation is one participant's consent.
The CASE_MANAGER takes the decision only from the Case Owner and only for an open proposal.
The Case Owner may decide without waiting for every participant to answer.
