# User Story `2026_034`

<!-- story-start -->As a Participant, I want whether a reply authorizes an action to follow from the case record and from a rule the protocol fixes for each kind of request, so that no Participant can extend its own authority and every Participant agrees on which actions were authorized<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

An action that needs another Participant's agreement is asked for, and the reply authorizes it.
If the asker, a deployment setting or a private working list could decide what a late or missing reply means, two Participants could hold different views of what was authorized.
Fixing the rule per kind of request, and reading the answer from the case record, gives every Participant the same answer.
