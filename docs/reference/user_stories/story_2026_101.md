# User Story `2026_101`

<!-- story-start -->As a Participant that tracks a case in its own internal systems, I want to tell the case which of my tracking IDs it goes by and change that set as my tracking changes, so that messages about the case reach the right ticket in my tracker and other participants can name that ticket when they contact me outside the case<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

Each organization in a case already has its own tracker, and a case often maps to more than one ticket in it, such as a PSIRT ticket and an engineering ticket.
The participant states its current set of tracking IDs on its participant status, and it may add or drop IDs as the case goes on.
Every participant in the case sees the set, so a coordinator can say "your SEC-1234 ticket" and reach the right person faster than by naming the case.
