# User Story `2026_090`

<!-- story-start -->As a Participant, I want to withdraw from the embargo in force by an explicit withdrawal, and my refusal of a proposed revision never to count as one, so that I can leave terms I can no longer keep and refusing new terms never releases me from the terms in force<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

A withdrawal is a `Leave` of the embargo in force, sent to the CASE_MANAGER; a `Reject` refuses proposed terms only.
Only a signatory other than the Case Owner can withdraw; the Case Owner ends an embargo by terminating it.
