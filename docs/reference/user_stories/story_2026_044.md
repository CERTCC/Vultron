# User Story `2026_044`

<!-- story-start -->As a Participant, I want to ask which actions are open to me in a case now, and why the others are not, so that I do not attempt a step that the case does not allow<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

Which actions a Participant may take depends on the case state, the Participant's own state and its roles.
Working that out by hand from the state machines is error-prone, and a refused action costs a round trip.
A machine-readable answer also lets an automated client choose its next step.
