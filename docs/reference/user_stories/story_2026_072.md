# User Story `2026_072`

<!-- story-start -->As a Participant, I want each ledger entry to hold the activity exactly as its asserter sent it, under the asserter's own name, so that the record shows who said what in their own words and never credits an act to an actor that did not make it<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

The entry keeps the received body without dropping, inlining or re-serializing any part of it, and the transport envelope that carried it stays separate.
The asserter named in the entry is the actor that sent the activity, never the CASE_MANAGER in its place.
An expiry that the CASE_MANAGER records is its own entry, not an activity attributed to the invitee that sent nothing.
