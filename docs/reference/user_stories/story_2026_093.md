# User Story `2026_093`

<!-- story-start -->As a Participant, I want an acceptance I send after my invitation's deadline still to count while the case can answer the embargo I accepted, and otherwise, when the case holds terms of its own, to bring me a fresh invitation to those terms, so that a late yes is never lost<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

An embargo is available when it is the embargo in force or an open proposal, and its end time has not passed.
A late acceptance of an available embargo is recorded as an on-time one is.
A late acceptance of an embargo the case held but that is no longer available is refused with the refusal notice.
When the case holds current terms, the notice comes with a fresh invitation to the embargo in force, or else to the earliest-expiring open proposal.
A late acceptance of an embargo the case never held gets a processing fault.
