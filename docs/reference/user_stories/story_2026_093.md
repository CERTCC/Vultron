# User Story `2026_093`

<!-- story-start -->As a Participant, I want an acceptance I send after my invitation's deadline still to count while the case can answer the embargo I accepted, and otherwise, when the case holds terms of its own, to bring me a fresh invitation to those terms, so that a late yes is never lost<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

The case can answer the embargo in force and its open proposals.
A late acceptance of an embargo the case can answer is recorded as an on-time one is.
A late acceptance of any other embargo is answered with a fresh invitation to the embargo in force, or else to the earliest-expiring open proposal.
With no embargo in force and none proposed, a late acceptance changes nothing.
