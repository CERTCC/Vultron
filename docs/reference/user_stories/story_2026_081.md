# User Story `2026_081`

<!-- story-start -->As a Reporter, I want to propose embargo terms when I submit a report, and to have the shorter of my terms and the Case Owner's default take effect at once while the longer stays open as a revision, so that the case is protected now and neither party's terms are lost<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

The Reporter's terms travel with the report and name the report until the case exists.
The shorter terms are the embargo in force from the start, at their stated length.
The longer terms are relayed as a revision proposal to the party whose terms took effect, like any other revision.
A report submitted while an embargo proposal is pending accepts that proposal's terms.
