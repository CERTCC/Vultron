# User Story `2026_040`

<!-- story-start -->As a Participant, I want the CASE_MANAGER either to record what I assert exactly as I sent it or to refuse it whole and tell me why, so that I know whether the case record holds my action and it never shows an action I did not take<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

Until the CASE_MANAGER records it, a Participant's message is an assertion, not a case event.
A partly recorded or silently changed assertion leaves the case showing something that nobody said, and a silent refusal leaves the sender believing its action took effect.
A refusal notice that names every reason lets the sender correct the assertion and try again.
