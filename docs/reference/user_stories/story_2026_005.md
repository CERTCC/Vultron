# User Story `2026_005`

<!-- story-start -->As a Participant, I want to keep an up-to-date record of the case, whatever order the messages arrive in, so that I do not have to log in to another organization's platform to see updates<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

In Vultron, each organization runs its own Actor, and Actors exchange ActivityStreams 2.0 (AS2) activities over ActivityPub.
The CASE_MANAGER coordinates the case, and each Participant keeps its own Participant Case Replica.
A Participant Case Replica that missed entries catches up from the ledger.
See [Case Ledger Synchronization](../../topics/case_lifecycle/case_ledger_sync.md) and [Annex E: ActivityPub](../vultron-spec/annex-e-activitypub.md).
