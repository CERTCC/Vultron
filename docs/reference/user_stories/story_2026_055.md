# User Story `2026_055`

<!-- story-start -->As a Participant, I want the CASE_MANAGER to give me a complete copy of each case I take part in, from the moment the case is introduced to me, so that I hold the whole case and not a fragment of it<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

A copy built from a reference, a stub or a partial payload leaves the Participant to find the rest elsewhere, and two Participants can find different things.
The case introduction carries the full case with its participant records inline, and later full copies replace or seed the local copy.
Until the case arrives, the Participant treats it as absent.
