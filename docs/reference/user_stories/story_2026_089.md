# User Story `2026_089`

<!-- story-start -->As a Participant, I want my agreement to an embargo to carry over to a revision that ends no later than it, and not to one that ends later, and a revision proposal to leave everyone's consent to the embargo in force as it is until the Case Owner activates the revision, so that I am never bound for longer than I agreed to<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

Until a revision is activated, the embargo in force stays in force and its signatories stay bound.
The proposer of a revision agrees to it by proposing it.
The CASE_MANAGER records each carried-over agreement in the case ledger, and every replica copies those entries.
