# User Story `2026_095`

<!-- story-start -->As a Participant, I want an embargo's ending to record why it ended, such as a ready fix, a deployed fix or another stated reason, and to close every proposal still open on it, so that every participant knows why the secrecy ended and nobody negotiates terms that no longer exist<!-- story-end -->

## Notes

An embargo ended early by an actor's own choice names its reason, and the reason is recorded as the termination's cause.
Ending the embargo writes no consent row: with no embargo in force, no row binds anyone.
