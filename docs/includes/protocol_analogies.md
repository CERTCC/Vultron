Each protocol below competes with centralized products that do the same job inside one company's walls.
The products work well until you need to reach someone who uses a different one.

| Protocol | What it lets you do | The centralized alternative |
|---|---|---|
| SMTP (email) | Send mail to anyone, whatever mail service they use | Direct messages inside one platform, such as Slack or Microsoft Teams |
| Extensible Messaging and Presence Protocol (XMPP) | Chat with people on servers run by other organizations | Single-vendor chat services, such as WhatsApp or Facebook Messenger |
| Really Simple Syndication (RSS) and Atom | Follow any site's updates in the reader you choose | A social media timeline that shows only what its platform hosts |
| Vultron | Coordinate a vulnerability case with partners, whatever tracker each of them runs | An account on every partner's coordination portal or disclosure platform |
