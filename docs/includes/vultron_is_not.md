Each statement of what Vultron is not comes with where your own system plugs in instead.

| Vultron is not… | Where your system plugs in |
|---|---|
| A tracking system, such as [Bugzilla](https://www.bugzilla.org/){:target="_blank"} or [Jira](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira){:target="_blank"} | Your tracker stays. It sends a Vultron message at each milestone its workflow already records: report received, validated, accepted, closed. See [Process Implementation Notes](../howto/process_implementation.md). |
| A CVD or threat coordination tool, such as the [Vulnerability Information and Coordination Environment (VINCE)](https://github.com/CERTCC/VINCE){:target="_blank"} or the [Malware Information Sharing Platform (MISP)](https://www.misp-project.org/){:target="_blank"} | Your coordination tool becomes a participant in the case, and can host cases for others by providing the Case Hosting capability set. See [Capability Sets](../reference/vultron-spec/index.md#122-capability-sets). |
| A vulnerability disclosure program, such as the [Department of Defense Cyber Crime Center (DC3) Vulnerability Disclosure Program (VDP)](https://www.dc3.mil/Missions/Vulnerability-Disclosure/Vulnerability-Disclosure-Program-VDP/){:target="_blank"} | Your program's policy stays yours. Its intake accepts reports as Vultron messages from any reporter's system. See [Submit a Report](../tutorials/submit-a-report.md). |
| A disclosure platform or service, such as [HackerOne](https://hackerone.com/){:target="_blank"}, [Bugcrowd](https://www.bugcrowd.com/){:target="_blank"}, or [Synack](https://www.synack.com/){:target="_blank"} | Your platform joins cases as a participant, so its customers can coordinate with parties who are not on it. See [Capability Sets](../reference/vultron-spec/index.md#122-capability-sets). |
| A prioritization tool, such as [Stakeholder-Specific Vulnerability Categorization (SSVC)](https://github.com/CERTCC/SSVC){:target="_blank"} or the [Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS)](https://www.first.org/cvss/){:target="_blank"} | Your prioritization scheme answers the prioritization call-out point, where the protocol asks whether to engage a case. See [Report Prioritization](../topics/capability_model/index.md#report-prioritization). |
| A product | Vultron is a feature set that products implement. Every decision it leaves open is a call-out point your own system answers. See the [Capability Model](../topics/capability_model/index.md). |
